# Infosec Overview

**Key Concepts:**

- **Information Security (Infosec):** The practice of protecting data from unauthorized access, use, or change. The core goal is to maintain the `Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) of data`.
- **Risk Management Process:** A five-step cycle (Identify, Analyze, Evaluate, Deal with, and Monitor) that organizations use to manage and mitigate risks to their business operations and data.
- **Red Team vs. Blue Team:** The `Red Team` simulates attackers (offensive security) to find weaknesses, while the `Blue Team` works to defend systems and respond to incidents (defensive security).

**Practical Application:**

- Penetration testers are a type of `Red Team` professional. They help organizations identify risks by finding and reporting vulnerabilities. To do this effectively, a tester must understand the risk management process to accurately rate the severity of their findings in the context of the client's business.
